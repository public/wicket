- Standing reach's words ([#657](https://github.com/public/wicket/issues/657)): the glossary defines
  **standing reach** (the feature), a **standing request** (a pending one, `s-…`) and a **standing
  rule** (an approved one), and the console reference describes its inbox card and the Always
  allowed card. `wicket standing`'s help and wicket's messages say "standing request" where they
  said "standing ask", and `ci/check-terms.sh` flags "standing ask". `request_access`'s reference
  lists its `standing` scope and the `methods`, `paths` and `dangerous` arguments.
