- A model call through the egress proxy (a driven Remote Control box's) is charged its real usage,
  like one through the model proxy: the model gate asks the upstream for an unencoded response (the
  box's `Accept-Encoding` got a compressed stream whose usage couldn't be read, so every call was
  charged its whole reservation), and sends the vetted body with its own length. When the budget
  left is below a request's `max_tokens`, an extended thinking budget at or above the lowered
  `max_tokens` is lowered below it, and a request that no valid lowering fits (fewer than 1025
  output tokens left with thinking enabled) is refused with the budget left and the thinking
  budget, instead of going upstream invalid
  ([#310](https://github.com/public/wicket/issues/310)).
