- `wicket wake` right after a control-host outage longer than an agent's claim (30 s) no longer
  starts a second box of the agent while its first still runs on its node: a lapsed claim is
  taken over only once none of the agent's runs is recorded running with authority left, plus 30 s
  for clocks, or once the reconciler has found the old box gone. The fence is in the ledger, with
  the claim, so it also holds on a standby restored from the state store
  ([#446](https://github.com/public/wicket/issues/446)).
