- A Vultr node that never comes up on the network no longer bills for 15 minutes before wicket gives
  up. `wicket place create` probes the node's port 22: once a connect is accepted or refused it
  waits for setup as before (up to 15 minutes); if every connect times out or is unreachable for
  `--give-up-silent` (default 4 minutes) it releases the instance, records a
  `placement.node_unreachable` audit record and says so, and the node's cost leaves the envelope.
  The reconciler does the same for a lease's node (`vultr.give_up_silent` in placement.yaml)
  ([#283](https://github.com/public/wicket/issues/283)).
