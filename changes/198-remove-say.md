- `wicket say` and the console's **Follow-up** box are gone: you work with a running agent in its
  session ([#198](https://github.com/public/wicket/issues/198)). In a browser or on a phone, that's
  the console's session page (#327). At the host's shell, it's the new `wicket session`. The
  console's signed `say` action is refused, with a pointer to the session.
